 \ex
{\code{DISCx26x10x13x003}}{40}
{
Consider all the 4 letter words you can make from the 5 vowels (i.e. A, E, I, O, U).

\begin{enumerate}

\item
What is the probability that your 4 letter word contains only A's and U's?

\item
What is the probability that your 4 letter word does {\bf not} contain any E's?


\item
What is the probability that your 4 letter word does contains at least one E?


\end{enumerate}

}{


}{}{type: HW}





 \ex
{\code{DISCx26x10x13x004}}{60}
{
A jar contains 3 red marbles, 4 blue marbles, and 5 yellow marbles.

\begin{enumerate}

\item Suppose you take 2 marbles, \emph{with replacement}.  
What is the probability that you take a red one first, and a yellow one second?

\item Suppose you take 2 marbles, \emph{without replacement}
What is the probability that you take a red one first, and a yellow one second?


\item  Suppose you take 3 marbles, without replacement.  What is the probability that all are red?

\item  Suppose you take 3 marbles, without replacement.  What is the probability that none are red?

\item  Suppose you take 3 marbles, without replacement.  What is the probability that at least one is red?

\end{enumerate}


}{


}{}{type: HW}
